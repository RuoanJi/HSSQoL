# Free battles (自由对战): the opponent pool

Generated by `make_freebattle.py` from the HSSQoL data dump (`freeBattle` section). Raw data: [`data/freebattle.json`](data/freebattle.json). Interactive version: [`freebattle.html`](freebattle.html).

## How an opponent is chosen

自由对战 is the Japanese **Net対戦** (`NetBattle` / `NetBattleManager` in the code). The opponents are **not generated**: each one is a recorded player fleet that ships with the game. At boot the game loads the pool table: a fleet file, its tier and a name (`AppData.freeBattleDataFileName_` / `freeBattleDataRank_` / `freeBattleDataUserName_`). There are **142 fleets** in **8 tiers**.

1. **Your tier** comes from your Net pirate rank (`NetBattleManager.GetFreeBattleRank`). The tier table is below.
2. **The deck.** The game keeps a shuffled queue of pool indexes (`AppData.freeBattleList_`). It builds a new one when the queue is empty or your tier changed. It holds every fleet of your tier, plus **one** random fleet from the tier below (if there is one) and **one** from the tier above. It leaves out the fleet you fought last (stored in the save), then shuffles.
3. **Each battle** takes the next fleet off the queue and unpacks it (`NetBattleData.CreateNetForce`). The fleet is a fixed snapshot: its crew and monsters are not scaled to you.
4. **It never runs out.** An empty queue is simply dealt again. The deck isn't kept in the save, so it's rebuilt after every load. Only the last opponent is remembered, so you never get the same fleet twice in a row.

## Net pirate rank

Your rank (`NetBattleManager.netBattleRank_`, starts at 1) is an exp bar fed by wins, not a plain win count. Decoded from `SubForm.ProNetBattleData` / `ProNetBattleStart` / `ProNetRankUp`.

- **Each 自由对战 win gives +1 Net exp** (`netBattleExp_`). Friend battles don't give exp.
- **Rank R needs 2R + 3 exp** (`GetNetRankNextExp = 2·rank + 3 − exp`). On reaching it, the rank goes up by 1 and exp resets to 0. A rank-up gives a reward (`AddResearchPoint` 10, probably medals).
- So reaching rank R from rank 1 takes **(R − 1)(R + 3) wins** in total.
- **Losing streaks:** a loss gives no exp but adds 1 to a losing streak (save slot 28), and any win resets the streak. Once the streak is at 5 (`FREE_BATTLE_LOSE_STREAK`), a loss drops you one rank (not below 1), with exp set to about old rank − 4 (not below 0). The streak doesn't seem to reset after a drop, so each further loss drops another rank until you win (read from the code, not tested).
- **Every 20th win in total** gives a bonus (`AddResearchPoint` 10, `FREE_BATTLE_WIN_BONUS = 20`).
- A weekly rank-up timer (`CheckRankUpTiming`, next Monday) is left over from the online version. Offline, exp alone moves the rank: a save with 84 wins and no losses sits at rank 8 with 7 exp, exactly 77 + 7.

## Where the ship appears

Net battles need event flag 5 (they open at 船团等级 20). The opponent ship spawns on the investigation spot (海域调查) of a sea area that **is not cleared** (`LandMap.netBattlePos_`). It picks one of those spots at random and avoids the previous position. It moves after every battle and when you change map.

If every sea area with an investigation spot were cleared, there would be no spot left, and the ship would stop appearing. This hasn't been seen in the game.
