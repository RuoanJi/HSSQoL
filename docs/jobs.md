## Knowledge cost of switching to every job / 转职所需知识总量

Switching to a job spends its required knowledge levels (and wipes the current partial EXP of those types). Knowledge level L → L+1 costs (L+1) × 1000 EXP (cap Lv 20), so earning a requirement of r levels from 0 costs 1000 × r(r+1)/2 EXP ("min EXP"; more if the levels are spent from higher up).
